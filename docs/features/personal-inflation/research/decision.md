# Locked product (29 Aug 2026)

**Not A.** A (state CPI dashboard only) is a data page. This feature is **your π** for real returns (12% SIP minus 2% vs minus 10%).

**Lived extras / “your inflation” is the feature.** Earlier “V0 = A” meant: don’t *label* a lease jump as MoSPI. It did **not** mean the idea is useless.

## What the tool is

1. **City** (→ State Urban official category rates). Not self-picked tier.  
2. **Short interview** (radios/chips/selects, **no sliders**) → **persona** (`w` + lived flags). Corpus-style chapters: household, then only the beats that household unlocked.  
3. **Weight engine:** `π = Σ w_i × π_i` (`w` sum to 100%).  
4. Lived **presets** on flagged lines (e.g. new lease → labelled **12**, not MoSPI 04.1 **~2%**).  
5. Apply **your estimate** to calculators (opt-in). Show official-weighted next to it so 2% vs 10% rent is visible.

Rupee **level** (₹50k vs ₹15k rent) still does not change π. **Rate** on that slice (10% vs 2%) and **share** of the basket do.

## Two knobs (both real)

| Knob | Example | In the engine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Share `w`** | Rent is 40% of my life, not 7% in HCES | Chapter 2 persona |
| **Rate `π_i`** | My lease reset vs MoSPI 04.1 ~2% | Chapter 3 flag → lived seed; **your estimate**, not “MoSPI rent” |

`2% inflation on 12% return` vs `10% inflation on 12% return` is **rate**, and it only hits the combined number if rent has **weight**. A 10% rent jump on a 7% share moves headline a little; on 40% share it moves a lot. That is why the weight engine is the crux.

## What we still refuse

- Calling the 10% **MoSPI**.  
- Hardcoding Aon 12% / listing 10% as the official line.  
- Spend bands as if rupees were inflation.  
- User-picked Tier 1.
