# Lived inflation vs official CPI

CPI is a **fixed-quality national sample**. A person can have **real** year-on-year cost growth that that sample **under-represents**. That does not make a newspaper “10% medical inflation” into MoSPI. It means the product has **two honest columns**.

## In the index (compare to official)

Food, fuel, piped utilities, clothing, **measured** rents in the CPI sample, many medicines/OPD as sampled, school **fees in the sample**, buses, some passenger transport, household help **as sampled**, recreation, restaurants, personal care.

Personalising here = **reweight** those published rates (see [weights-kids-wealth.md](weights-kids-wealth.md)).

## Real for a household, weakly or not in “the number they see on TV”

These are the items easy to forget if we only copy MoSPI:

| Lived item | Why it hits a normal urban household | How we might factor (research, not PRD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health insurance age-band** | Premia jump at 41, 51, … even if MoSPI health YoY is ~1–2% | Q5 private cover → **lived** health flag; do not paste Aon 12% as CPI |
| **Diagnostics, dental, mental health, cashless denial** | Chains and OP not equal to CPI health mix | Same Q5; weight health + wider lived band |
| **New lease / brokerage vs sitting rent** | CPI tracks sampled rents; your jump is contractual | Q1 renter + Q4 rent → wider **rent band**, not 10% overlay |
| **Society CAM, parking, club, genset, tanker, RO** | Apartment India; CPI housing is rent + some dwelling services | Renter/owner-in-society: nudge housing/utilities **share**; lived housing band |
| **Coaching, edtech, international school** | JEE/NEET spend is an industry CPI barely names | Q3 senior/coaching → education **share** + lived education band |
| **Creche / nanny beyond “domestic help” sample** | Two-income + infants | Fold into help/education shares if Q3/Q4 say so |
| **Ride-hail surge, quick-commerce fees** | Dynamic prices | Q4 dining & travel or commute implied by outflow; lived transport band — no Ola index |
| **FX + foreign university / vacation** | Rupee and foreign tuition | Q6 often + Q3 college abroad later; **lifestyle vs lived FX** — do not mix into baseline SIP rate by default |
| **Vehicle insurance, PUC, parking, FASTag** | Own-car India | If we ever ask commute (not in the six): 07.2 weight; else leave in essentials |
| **Pets, fertility, special-needs care** | Large and real; rare in a 6-Q form | **Out of questionnaire.** Too sparse. User can put under Q4 “other” / health |
| **Platform subscriptions** | OTT, cloud, mobile | Partly in communication/recreation CPI; not a 7th question |
| **Gold, MF expense ratio, loan interest** | Assets / debt | **Not consumption inflation.** EMI is **share of outflow** (Q1/Q4), not an inflation rate |

## What we still do *not* treat as inflation

- Buying a bigger fridge or a 1200cc bike (**lifestyle**).  
- Term insurance premium (protection, not CPI).  
- Stamp duty, one-time brokerage (level, not annual π).  
- Tax slab.

## Product implication

Show **official-weighted π** as the comparable number. Show **lived** as a range or “also in your costs” list driven by Q3/Q5/Q1 renter — **research must pick the band method** (fixed pp vs qualitative). Do not average fake 12% into the calculator rate until a PRD says so.
