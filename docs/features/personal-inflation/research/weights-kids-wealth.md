# Weights, children, and wealth

**Rupees do not change inflation.** Shares change a **combined** π only. Full split: [inflation-vs-weights.md](inflation-vs-weights.md).

Personalising **does** change **shares** of the basket. It does **not** stack 5% + 3% per child. Two people with the same shares get the same π at ₹40k or ₹1.5L.

- Education CPI is one (or a few **group**) published rates. Two children ≈ **more rupees** in that slice, so **higher weight**, so `π` moves **if** that slice’s rate differs from the rest.  
- Weights **sum to 100%**. If education doubles as a share, something else shrinks **or** total spend rises (savings take it).  
- There is **no** official “raising a child 3%” series. Extra food/help/health sit in those divisions.  
- Class 1 vs JEE: **share + mix** (and often **lifestyle**). General cut: school vs senior/coaching — see [questionnaire.md](questionnaire.md) Q3. Not crores of permutations.  
- **Wealth** is the rupee bill and SIP/corpus, not a fake double-digit `π`.  

Toy: education 5%, rest 2% → 10% of spend on education ≈ 2.3% `π`; 20% of spend ≈ 2.6% `π`. The **fee line** can double. That is the point of the ledger.
