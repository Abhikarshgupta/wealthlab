# Audit of personal-inflation research

**Persona:** senior tax planner / CA / financial advisor (not a product manager).  
**Date of audit:** 29 August 2026.  
**Scope:** every file under `docs/features/personal-inflation/` as of that date, checked against published government and independent series — not against the folder’s own wording.

**Verdict in one line:** directionally honest about not faking MoSPI; **not fit to freeze**. For **metros / colloquial T1–3** the slice is the right product. Do not sell it as “CPI is a lie.” Completeness of official series and engine hygiene (owners, EMI, jewellery, **State Urban**) still fail.

**Scope addendum (same day):** product is **not** for rural / non-user towns. See **[scope-urban-metros.md](scope-urban-metros.md)**.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Large share of *India*? | **No, and that is now out of scope.** Rural majority dropped by design. |
| Large share of *WealthLab cities* (Mumbai, Kochi, Chandigarh, Jammu, Guwahati, Lucknow, Kota, Vadodara, …)? | **Yes, if labelled.** HCES all-urban mean ₹~24k/HH is the **average pie**, not the user’s ledger. Showcase **shares** (rent 40% vs HCES 6.58%), not “24k is fake.” |
| Gov number “nowhere close to real life”? | **Mix and lease rates can diverge. CPI is still the average basket.** Copy that says MoSPI is false is a fail. |
| North East different? | **Levels yes; official Urban YoY recently often *lower* (Assam Urban 2.26% vs all-India Urban 3.92%, June 2026).** Use State Urban, no NE overlay. |
| All government / credible metrics? | **No.** State/UT Urban is now **mandatory** for this scope, still missing from research notes. |
