## What was **not** found (do not invent)

- No all-India **city** CPI under MoSPI general CPI 2024 (State/UT yes; CPI-IW centres yes).
- No IRDAI “medical inflation index”.
- No official maid-wage CPI, Ola/Uber CPI, or UGC national fee CPI.
- No current Census 2021 urbanisation table (Census not released as of this audit).
